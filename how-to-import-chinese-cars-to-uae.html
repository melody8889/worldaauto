How to Import Chinese Cars to the UAE

A practical checklist for UAE dealers, fleet buyers and importers sourcing new and used vehicles from China.

Who this guide is for

This guide is for UAE car dealers, fleet operators and importers buying Chinese vehicles for Dubai, Abu Dhabi and other Emirates. It covers passenger cars, SUVs, luxury vehicles, electric vehicles and selected commercial trucks.

Typical costs and delivery time

Vehicle prices vary by brand, model, year, mileage and specification. As a planning range, wholesale buyers should request a vehicle price plus an itemized shipping quote rather than relying on a single “landed price.” RoRo or container shipping from a Chinese port to the UAE commonly takes about 20–35 days after the vehicle is ready; port congestion, vessel schedules and customs clearance can change the final timing.

Documents normally required

  • Commercial invoice and sales contract
  • Packing list or vehicle details sheet
  • Export customs documentation
  • Bill of lading
  • Vehicle registration or title documents, where applicable
  • Inspection report and VIN information

Import and registration considerations

Confirm UAE customs duty, VAT, vehicle age rules, conformity requirements and registration requirements before payment. Electric vehicles may require additional battery, safety or technical information. The buyer or local customs agent should confirm the latest rules for the destination Emirate.

How the process works

  1. Confirm model, quantity, specification and destination port.
  2. Compare available stock and receive inspection photos or video.
  3. Approve the vehicle and commercial invoice.
  4. Complete payment and export documentation.
  5. Arrange RoRo or container shipping, then customs clearance and registration in the UAE.
