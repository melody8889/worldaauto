About Us

Worlda Global Auto is not a regular car dealer.

Worlda Global Auto supplies new cars, used cars and electric trucks to overseas dealers, importers and distributors. We support vehicle sourcing, stock verification, export document coordination, and sea or land shipment planning for markets across Russia, the Middle East, Central Asia and Africa.

FAQ

Export Questions

Which types of buyers do you work with?

We work with overseas vehicle dealers, importers, distributors, fleet operators and automotive trading companies. We support both trial orders and larger wholesale orders, depending on the vehicle model and destination market.

What vehicles can you supply?

We supply Chinese brand new cars, international brand vehicles, used cars, luxury vehicles and electric trucks. Availability depends on the model, configuration, production year and current stock.

Can I inspect the car before shipment?

Yes. You may come to Khorgos port to inspect vehicles in person before loading. Our on-site colleague will accompany you to check the cars, take videos and confirm their condition. If you cannot visit, we can provide full walkaround videos, VIN photos and loading footage for verification.

What if there is a customs inspection at Khorgos?

We work with experienced local customs brokers at Khorgos. We fully cooperate with customs inspections and keep you updated throughout the process.

Which shipping methods and markets do you support?

We support sea freight and TIR land transport depending on the destination, quantity, delivery schedule and customs route. Our main service markets include Russia, the Middle East, Central Asia, Africa and other regions.

How can I start a wholesale inquiry?

Please send us the target model, model year, quantity, destination country or port and your expected purchase time. Our team will check availability and reply with vehicle options, pricing and shipping information.
