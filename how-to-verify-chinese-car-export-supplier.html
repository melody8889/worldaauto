How to Verify a Chinese Car Export Supplier

A buyer checklist for overseas dealers and importers comparing Chinese vehicle exporters before placing an order.

Why supplier verification matters

A professional exporter should be able to explain vehicle identity, condition, price basis, documents, shipping and after-sales communication. A polished website alone is not enough; buyers should verify each part of the transaction.

1. Verify the company identity

  • Check that the company name, domain, email and phone number are consistent.
  • Ask for a business registration or export-company profile where appropriate.
  • Confirm the company’s office, warehouse or sourcing network through a video call.
  • Look for a traceable history of vehicles, shipments or customer references.

2. Verify the vehicle

  • Request the VIN and match it across photos, inspection records and documents.
  • Ask for a current walk-around video showing the date and vehicle identity.
  • For used cars, confirm mileage, accident history, service records and battery health where relevant.
  • Confirm exact specification, drive side, language, charging standard and destination compatibility.

3. Verify the quotation

Ask whether the price is EXW, FOB, CFR or CIF. The quotation should identify the model, year, quantity, currency, included services, payment schedule, validity period and excluded destination charges. A very low price with unclear specifications is a reason to request more evidence, not an automatic bargain.

4. Verify documents and payment

Confirm the commercial invoice, export documentation, bill of lading and ownership or registration documents required for your market. The beneficiary name on the bank account should be explainable and consistent with the contracting company. Use a written contract and keep inspection, payment and shipping records.

5. Compare service and communication

A reliable supplier answers specific questions, explains limitations and provides updates when stock or shipping changes. Ask who will handle inspection, port delivery, document coordination and shipment tracking. Confirm the contact person and escalation channel before sending a deposit.

FAQ

Should I pay before seeing the vehicle?

Request current photos or video, VIN information and a written quotation first. For used vehicles, arrange an independent or exporter-provided inspection before final approval.

What information should I include in an enquiry?

Include model, year, new or used condition, quantity, destination country or port, specification, budget and preferred shipping term.
