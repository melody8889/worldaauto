China Car Shipping to Saudi Arabia: Cost, Documents and Time

A practical guide for Saudi dealers and importers planning vehicle purchases, shipping and customs clearance from China.

Who this guide is for

This guide is for Saudi vehicle dealers, importers, fleet buyers and businesses comparing Chinese cars, SUVs, luxury models and electric vehicles for Jeddah, Dammam or other Saudi destinations.

What determines the total cost?

Your total landed cost normally includes the vehicle price, inland transport to the Chinese port, export charges, ocean freight, marine insurance, destination port charges, customs duty, VAT, clearance and local registration. Ask for a written quote that separates FOB, freight and destination costs. Exact amounts depend on model, engine or battery type, value, dimensions and destination port.

Shipping methods and timing

RoRo is often suitable for standard passenger cars and SUVs. Container shipping can be useful for high-value vehicles or consolidated cargo. A planning estimate for ocean transit from China to Saudi Arabia is commonly around 25–40 days, followed by customs and registration time. The exporter should confirm the sailing schedule before booking.

Documents to prepare

  • Commercial invoice and purchase contract
  • Vehicle identification and inspection records
  • Export declaration and customs documents
  • Bill of lading
  • Certificate of origin, if required
  • Documents required by the Saudi importer, customs broker or registration authority

Saudi compliance questions

Before ordering, confirm vehicle age limits, conformity or certification requirements, right-hand or left-hand drive rules, fuel and emissions requirements, electric-vehicle battery information, and the latest customs and VAT treatment. Requirements can change, so the importer and Saudi customs broker should verify the current rules.

FAQ

Which Saudi ports can be used?

Jeddah Islamic Port and King Abdulaziz Port in Dammam are common options, subject to vessel schedule and cargo type.

Yes. Send the model, quantity and destination port and we can prepare an itemized quotation.
